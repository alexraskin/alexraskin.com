## Hi there!

I'm **Alexander (Alex) Raskin**. Thanks for stopping by my little corner of the internet!

I'm a self-taught DevOps engineer who loves building things, taking them apart, and figuring out how they work. I've always enjoyed learning by doing.

Most of my projects involve **Go, Terraform, and more recently, Nix**. I tend to have just as much fun building the infrastructure as I do building the projects themselves.

My [homelab](https://github.com/alexraskin/infrastructure) consists of a 9-node Kubernetes cluster running on [Talos](https://www.talos.dev/). All nine nodes live on a single Proxmox box, so it's highly available in theory and a single point of failure in practice.

My daily driver is a MacBook Air, configured with [Nix](https://github.com/alexraskin/nix-config), which I'm always finding excuses to tinker with.

Away from the terminal, I'm a bit of a transportation nerd with a love for trains, planes, and cars. I enjoy spending time with my cat [Cosmo](/cosmo/) and probably play a little too much Reinhardt.

This site is a little collection of things I enjoy, projects I've worked on, and things I've learned along the way.
